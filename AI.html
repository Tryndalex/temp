AI

We combine practical AI with solid web foundations to create useful digital experiences, automation and content that can be understood by both people and modern search systems.

AI-ready web content

AI visibility is not a separate replacement for SEO. Strong fundamentals still matter: accessible pages, crawlable content, clear entities, useful information, descriptive titles and trustworthy sources.

AI integration

AI can be integrated into websites for practical tasks such as content workflows, assistance, classification, search and automation.

Clear information

Well-structured pages with explicit headings, concise answers and useful context make information easier for both people and automated systems to interpret.

Crawler access

If you want your public content discovered by AI search systems, review robots.txt and firewall rules. For ChatGPT Search, OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot as the crawler used for search discovery.

Structured information

Relevant Schema.org structured data can help search engines understand entities and page content. It should accurately represent information visible on the page.

AI without shortcuts

There is no special HTML tag that guarantees visibility in AI search. The practical approach is to publish useful, original and accessible content, maintain strong technical SEO and allow the relevant crawlers to access the pages you want discovered.
